The Dinajpur Zilla School Scout Group has trained young citizens in character, craft and community for over five decades. The white lotus, the open book and the burning candle on our crest are not decoration — they are the oath, repeated in front of every new intake.

The CrestThree Symbols, One Oath

✿

The White Lotus

Purity of intent. A scout keeps a clean word and a clean conscience, in the troop and out of it.

📖

The Open Book

Knowledge, kept open. Every badge is a small proof of something learned properly, not memorised.

🕯

The Burning Candle

Service that costs something. The candle gives light by spending itself — so does a scout.
